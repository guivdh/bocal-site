    Privacy

    No account, no ads, no trackers.

    Bocal keeps your links, nothing else. No ad profile, no e-mail to give, no password to remember.

    A first name is enough

    Just a first name, so the others know who added what. No e-mail, no password.

    No ads, no cookies

    No analytics, no trackers. Not in the app, not on this site.

    Your data stays in Europe

    Jars and links are hosted in the European Union. Nothing is sold.

    Erase everything, in one tap

    “Delete my data” erases your first name and your place in your jars, from the app's settings.

    Questions

    Everything you might wonder.

    Is it free?

    Yes. Bocal is free, with no ads.

    Do I need an account?

    No. A first name is enough: no e-mail, no password.

    Do my friends need the app?

    Yes, to see the jar and add links to it. Send them the invite link: it takes them straight into the jar.

    What links can I keep?

    Any web link: an Instagram, TikTok or YouTube video, a recipe, a listing, an article, a restaurant's page…

    If I change phones, do I lose everything?

    No. Install Bocal, tap “I already have jars”, enter a jar's code and pick your first name: you get your place and your links back.
