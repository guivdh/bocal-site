Delete my data

Bocal has no account: a first name and an identifier created at random on your phone are enough. You can erase everything yourself, at any time.

From the app

  • Open Bocal.
  • Tap your initial, at the top right of the home screen.
  • Tap Delete my data, then Delete everything.

It's immediate and permanent: your first name, your identifier and your places in the jars are erased. The links you added to shared jars stay there for the other members, without your name. If you were the admin of a jar, the role goes to the longest-standing member; a jar with no members left is deleted with its links.

Without the app

Already uninstalled Bocal? Write to contact@g-vandenherrewegen.be with your first name in Bocal and the name of one of your jars: we erase your data within one month and confirm it to you.

Without doing anything, an identifier that hasn't been in any jar for 30 days is erased automatically.
