The service

Bocal lets you create “jars” (shared lists of links), invite other people to them with a code, and keep links in them together. Bocal is free, with no ads.

Bocal works without an account: a first name is enough. To get your jars back on another phone, you need the code of each of them.

  • You put nothing illegal in it: hateful content, harassment, sexual content involving minors, invasion of other people's privacy, counterfeiting, malware, scams…
  • You don't pretend to be someone else. “Who are you?” is for getting your place back, not another member's.
  • You don't try to get around Bocal's protections, guess codes or overload the service.

Invite codes

Anyone who has a jar's code can join it, see its links and members, and take over a member's place. Only share your codes with people you trust. If a code has spread too far, any member of the jar can create a new one (“New code”): the old one stops working.
